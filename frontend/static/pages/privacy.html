Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains how Interview Coach Prep (ICP) collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We collect your name and email address for account management and security.

Professional Data: We collect resume files and interview responses that you voluntarily upload or provide to our AI engine.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to provide AI-driven feedback, personalise your experience, maintain account security, and improve our services through anonymised analysis.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share necessary information with:

  • AI Services: To process your resumes and interview responses.
  • Email Services: To send verification and reset links.
  • Database Services: To securely store your profile and history.
4. Data Security

We implement technical and organisational security measures to protect your data, including encryption for passwords and secure API communication.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage your profile settings or use the Delete Account feature to permanently remove all your data from our systems.

6. Cookies

We use local storage and essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for third-party advertising.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 15. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 15.

For questions or data deletion requests, contact us via GitHub.
